04

Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act, 2023, you hold comprehensive rights over your personal data:

1. Right to Access (Section 11)

You can request a summary of personal data being processed by us, identities of any third-party processors, and processing details.

2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12)

You can request correction of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete data, updating of data, or erasure of personal data no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.

3. Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4))

You may withdraw your consent at any time as easily as it was given, with no impact on the legality of prior processing. You can adjust cookie preferences via the link in the footer.

4. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

You have the right to register grievances with our designated Grievance Officer regarding the processing of your personal data.

5. Right to Nominate (Section 14)

You may nominate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, will exercise your data principal rights.

To exercise any of your rights: Send an email to manjuacs87@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP Rights Request - [Your Name]". We will acknowledge and process your request within statutory timelines.
