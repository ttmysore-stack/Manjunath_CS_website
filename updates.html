Companies Act & MCA July 20, 2026

Annual MCA Filings Calendar & Compliance Reminders for FY 2025-26

Mandatory due dates for filing Financial Statements (AOC-4 / AOC-4 XBRL) and Annual Returns (MGT-7 / MGT-7A) for Private Limited Companies and OPCs.

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) requires all registered companies to file financial statements within 30 days of the AGM and Annual Return within 60 days of the AGM. Ensure timely board meetings, statutory audit report compilation, and Director signatures to avoid additional late filing fees.

Director & Annual Compliance July 15, 2026

DIR-3 KYC & e-KYC Annual Filing Deadline Notice

All Director Identification Number (DIN) holders must complete their annual DIR-3 KYC verification to maintain active DIN status.

Every individual holding a DIN as on 31st March must submit Form DIR-3 KYC or DIR-3 KYC-WEB annually. Failure to submit within the prescribed timeline leads to DIN deactivation and a mandatory statutory penalty of Rs. 5,000 upon reactivating.

Companies Act & MCA July 10, 2026

Dematerialisation of Shares & Half-Yearly PAS-6 Compliance for Private Companies

Mandatory issuance of securities in dematerialized form and half-yearly reconciliation PAS-6 returns for non-small private companies.

In accordance with Rule 9B of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, unlisted private companies (other than small companies) must facilitate dematerialisation of all existing securities and file Form PAS-6 within 60 days from the conclusion of each half-year.

IBBI Guidelines on Timelines in Liquidation & CIRP Proceedings

Recent IBBI circulars specifying revised timelines for claim verification, stakeholder committee meetings, and asset distribution.

LLP Compliance Updates June 28, 2026

LLP Form 11 Annual Return & Form 8 Statement of Accounts Reminder

Annual statutory compliance requirements for Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) registered under the LLP Act, 2008.

All LLPs registered in India must file Form 11 (Annual Return) by 30th May and Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) by 30th October each financial year. Late filing incurs a daily penalty of Rs. 100 per day till compliance is completed.

Quarterly disclosures and financial reporting requirements for real estate developers and registered promoter entities.
