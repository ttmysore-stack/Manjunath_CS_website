  • ID & address proof of members
  • Registered office proof
  • List of subscribers (minimum 10 producers)

Proprietorship to OPC (One Person Company)

Introduction

A Proprietorship is the simplest form of business owned and managed by a single

person. To obtain separate legal status, limited liability, and better business credibility,

a proprietor may shift the business into an OPC under the Companies Act, 2013.

Practically, there is no direct statutory conversion. A new OPC is incorporated and the

existing business of the proprietorship is transferred to the OPC.

Features of OPC

  • Separate legal entity
  • Limited liability protection
  • Single shareholder structure
  • Perpetual succession
  • Mandatory nominee concept
  • Governed by Companies Act, 2013
  • Better credibility with banks and investors

Advantages

  • Protection of personal assets
  • Corporate identity and branding
  • Easier bank funding and investments
  • Better business continuity
  • Tax planning opportunities
  • Increased customer confidence
  • Suitable for startups and solo founders

Documents Required

Proprietor Documents

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport size photo
  • Mobile number & email ID

Registered Office Proof

  • Rental agreement/ownership proof
  • Electricity bill or utility bill

OPC Incorporation Documents

  • DSC of director
  • Nominee consent (INC-3)
  • MOA & AOA
  • Declaration and consent forms

Business Transfer Documents

  • Business Transfer Agreement
  • List of assets and liabilities
  • GST details
  • Bank account details

GST Registration (Including Amendments & LUT)

Introduction

GST Registration is a tax registration required under the Goods and Services Tax Act for

businesses engaged in supply of goods or services, including website-based businesses

and e-commerce activities.

Why It Is Done

  • To comply with GST laws
  • To collect and remit GST
  • To claim Input Tax Credit (ITC)
  • Mandatory for interstate and many online transactions

Documents Required

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address proof of business
  • Bank proof/cancelled cheque
  • Photograph
  • Incorporation documents
  • Authorization letter

For Amendments

  • Supporting proof for changes
  • Board resolution (if company)

For LUT

  • GST certificate
  • PAN
  • Authorized signatory details

Benefits

  • Legal tax compliance
  • Input tax credit benefit
  • Increased credibility
  • Required for B2B dealings
  • Easier interstate business

Validity

  • GST registration: Lifetime until cancelled
  • LUT: Valid for one financial year and renewed annually
