PRODUCER COMPANY

Governed by Companies Act, 2013 (Chapter XXIA

Meaning

A Producer Company is a company formed by farmers/producers to carry on activities related to production, harvesting, procurement, marketing of primary produce.

Features

📂 Documents Required (Incorporation – SPICe+)

  • Separate legal entity
  • Minimum 10 individuals (producers) OR 2 producer institutions
  • Limited liability
  • Only producers can be members
  • No public issue of shares
  • Objective must relate to primary produce
  • DSC & DIN of directors
  • Name approval (must include “Producer Company Limited”)
  • MOA (object restricted to producer activities)
  • AOA
  • Declaration by professionals (INC-8)
  • DIR-2 (consent of directors)
  • ID & address proof of members
  • Registered office proof
  • List of subscribers (minimum 10 producers)

  • Notarised & apostilled documents

  • Bank account (FDI inflow)
  • KYC from AD Bank

Proprietorship to OPC (One Person Company)

Introduction

A Proprietorship is the simplest form of business owned and managed by a single person. To obtain separate legal status, limited liability, and better business credibility, a proprietor may shift the business into an OPC under the Companies Act, 2013. Practically, there is no direct statutory conversion. A new OPC is incorporated and the existing business of the proprietorship is transferred to the OPC.

Features of OPC

  • Separate legal entity
  • Limited liability protection
  • Single shareholder structure
  • Perpetual succession
  • Mandatory nominee concept
  • Governed by Companies Act, 2013
  • Better credibility with banks and investors

Advantages

  • Protection of personal assets
  • Corporate identity and branding
  • Easier bank funding and investments
  • Better business continuity
  • Tax planning opportunities
  • Increased customer confidence
  • Suitable for startups and solo founders

Documents Required

Proprietor Documents

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport size photo
  • Mobile number & email ID

Registered Office Proof

  • Rental agreement/ownership proof
  • Electricity bill or utility bill

OPC Incorporation Documents

  • DSC of director
  • Nominee consent (INC-3)
  • MOA & AOA
  • Declaration and consent forms

Business Transfer Documents

  • Business Transfer Agreement
  • List of assets and liabilities
  • GST details
  • Bank account details

Partnership Firm to LLP

Introduction

A Partnership Firm can be converted into a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) under the LLP Act, 2008. LLP combines the flexibility of a partnership with the benefit of limited liability and separate legal entity status. This is a statutory conversion under the LLP Act.

Features of LLP

  • Separate legal entity
  • Limited liability of partners
  • Flexible management structure
  • Perpetual succession
  • Less compliance compared to companies
  • No minimum capital requirement

Advantages

  • Protection against personal liability
  • Lower compliance burden
  • Better operational flexibility
  • Easy partner management
  • Suitable for professional firms and SMEs
  • Tax-efficient structure
  • No restriction on profit sharing

Documents Required

Firm Documents

  • Partnership Deed
  • Registration Certificate of firm (if registered)
  • PAN of firm
  • Latest IT Returns

Partner Documents

  • PAN & Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Passport size photos
  • Consent to act as designated partner

Financial Documents

  • Statement of Assets & Liabilities certified by CA
  • List of creditors
  • Consent from creditors

Office Proof

  • Rent agreement/ownership proof
  • Utility bill

GST Registration (Including Amendments & LUT)

Introduction

GST Registration is a tax registration required under the Goods and Services Tax Act for businesses engaged in supply of goods or services, including website-based businesses and e-commerce activities.

Why It Is Done

  • To comply with GST laws
  • To collect and remit GST
  • To claim Input Tax Credit (ITC)
  • Mandatory for interstate and many online transactions

Documents Required

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address proof of business
  • Bank proof/cancelled cheque
  • Photograph
  • Incorporation documents
  • Authorization letter

For Amendments

  • Supporting proof for changes
  • Board resolution (if company)

For LUT

  • GST certificate
  • PAN
  • Authorized signatory details

Benefits

  • Legal tax compliance
  • Input tax credit benefit
  • Increased credibility
  • Required for B2B dealings
  • Easier interstate business

Validity

  • GST registration: Lifetime until cancelled
  • LUT: Valid for one financial year and renewed annually
